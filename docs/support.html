Common questions

Why can't I swipe for my friend?

Because they haven't approved you yet. Making someone an account sends them a wingman request; until they accept it, they don't appear in the dropdown at the top of the deck. If they're signed in on the same phone, switch to their account from the avatar in the top corner and approve it there.

My friend approved me and then changed their mind.

That's allowed. Anyone can stop being someone's wingman, or take the permission back, from the People tab. You're returned to your own deck straight away.

Adding a photo does nothing.

Tap the tile with the plus on it directly — it's the picker itself. If you see a message about storage being blocked, the browser or device is refusing to save data locally; private browsing mode is the usual cause. In the installed app this doesn't happen.

Where did my profiles go?

Everything is stored on the device, so clearing the app's data, deleting the app, or clearing your browser's site data removes it. There's no cloud copy to restore from — that's the trade-off for the app not having a server.

How do I delete everything?

Settings (the cog, top right) → Reset everything. That erases every account, profile, photo, match and occasion on the device, immediately and permanently.

Are the other people real?

No. Everyone outside the accounts you create is a fictional character bundled with the app. Wingman isn't connected to a live dating pool, and nothing you do reaches another person.

Is my data used for anything?

No. There's no server to send it to. See the privacy policy.
