🪽 Wingman

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Wingman does not collect your data, because Wingman has nowhere to send it. There is no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you type, add or swipe stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Profiles, preferences, swipes, matches, occasions and connections are saved in your device's local storage, under a single key named wingman.state.v1.
  • Photos you add are resized on your device and saved in your device's IndexedDB database. They are never uploaded.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The app makes no network requests. It works with airplane mode on, which is the simplest way to verify this for yourself.

Photo library and camera

If you add a photo, iOS asks your permission to open your photo library or camera. The app reads only the pictures you pick, resizes them, and stores them locally. It does not read your library otherwise, and it does not use location data attached to your photos.

Other people's information

Wingman lets you build a profile for a friend or family member. Anything you enter about another person is stored on your device exactly like your own information, is never transmitted, and is deleted along with their profile. Please only create a profile for someone who is happy for you to do so — the app requires them to approve you before you can act on their behalf.

The other profiles in the app

Everyone outside the accounts you create is a fictional character bundled with the app. They are not real people, and no information about you reaches them.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Reset everything inside the app. That erases every account, profile, photo, match and message on the device immediately and permanently. Deleting the app does the same.

Children

Wingman is for people aged 18 and over, and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version of Wingman adds a server or accounts, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here.
