🪽 Wingman

Be the reason they finally meet.

A matchmaking app you use for other people. You get your own account and swipe for yourself — and when a friend approves you, you can swipe for them too.

Open Wingman → Download the single file

How it works
  • Ask a friend to let you be their wingman. They approve, and they appear in your dropdown.
  • Every card carries a compatibility score out of 100 that shows its working.
  • Give someone a real occasion — a wedding, a double date — and the deck re-scores for it.
  • When the person in front of you isn't right, their friends are one tap away.

Private by design

No account, no server, no analytics. Every profile, photo, swipe and match stays on your phone, and the app makes no network requests at all — you can check that in airplane mode.
